Pass-through charges applicable to your premises are scheduled to change from the 1 October 2025. The change in
these charges is dependent on the DUoS ﴾Distribution use of system﴿ or the ‘DG’ number that has been allocated to your
commercial premises.

Pass-through charges are the charges that all electricity suppliers must pay other entities in the Integrated Single
Electricity Market ﴾I-SEM﴿, when supplying electricity to customers, such as those incurred for the use of ESB Networks’
and Eirgrid’s systems. Some elements are not Flogas charges, these are market charges. Usually ESB Networks and
Eirgrid set these charges, subject to regulatory approval by the
Commission for Regulation of Utilities ﴾CRU﴿, on an annual basis.

Pass-through charges include:
 Transmission Use of Systems ﴾TUoS﴿
 Distribution Use of Systems ﴾DUoS﴿
 Market Operator Charges
 Imperfection Charges
 Electricity Tax
 MIC Charges
 PSO Levy
 Low Power Factor Surcharge

To understand more about pass-through charges for your business please visit
www.flogas.ie/business/pass-through-charges/

The 2025/2026 PSO Calculations:*
The PSO levy for the year the 1 October 2025 to the
30 September 2026 is set to a monthly charge and has been reduced to €2.01 and €7.77 for domestic and small commercial
customers, respectively. For medium/large commercial customers the monthly charge is set to fall to €0.96/KVA.
The PSO levy figures noted here exclude VAT.
*Changes to the PSO for 25/26 will appear on your invoices from 31 October 2025.
