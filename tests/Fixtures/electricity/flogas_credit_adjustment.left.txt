  Energy Saving hints and tips
• These days we use many electrical devices & appliances to get through our day. It would be difficult for any of
  us to stop using them, but making small changes to our habits can help to reduce energy consumption over
  time & being a bit smarter in the times we use devices can help to lessen use at peak times.
• Switch off unused devices at the wall socket when you are not using them. When devices are sleeping or in
  standby mode they still use small amounts of electricity. Timers or smart plugs can be a good way to do this.
