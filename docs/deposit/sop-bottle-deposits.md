# Bottle deposits at the till

*Draft for BookStack. Owner: check the shelf wording and who to ask, then publish.*

## What it is

Some glass bottles and jars from Udea carry a deposit: juices, milk and
yoghurt in glass, mineral water in glass, some jars of pasta, nuts and rice.
The customer pays the deposit when they buy the item and gets it back when
they bring the empty bottle or jar back.

| Deposit | Typical items |
|---|---|
| €0.25 | 200 ml – 1.5 l glass bottles, 500 g yoghurt jars |
| €0.70 | 1 l dairy bottles, large Wisselwaar jars |
| €0.10 | small beer and soda bottles |

## Selling: nothing to do

The till adds the deposit by itself. When you scan the item, a line
**"Bottle deposit 0.25"** (or 0.70 / 0.10) appears directly under it, and it
follows the quantity. It is printed on the receipt.

If a deposit line looks wrong, remove the **product** line, not the deposit:
the deposit goes with it. If you delete just the deposit line, it comes back
by itself.

## Returns

1. In the catalogue, open **Bottle Deposits**.
2. Tap the refund button for the size, **once per bottle or jar**:
   "Bottle deposit refund 0.25", "… 0.70" or "… 0.10".
3. If the customer is also buying something, the refund comes off their total.
   If they are only returning bottles, pay the refund out in **cash**.

Accept only clean, undamaged Udea bottles and jars of the kinds we sell with
a deposit. The deposit amount is the one on our shelf label for that item.
If you are not sure, do not refund: ask.

## Who to ask

The manager on duty, or the owner.
